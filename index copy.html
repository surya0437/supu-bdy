A Few Words

For You, Only You

Break the seal

My love,

There are days when I reach for the right words and find them frustratingly small. But today -- your birthday -- I won't let that stop me from trying.


You walked into my life and rearranged it, quietly and completely. The ordinary became extraordinary. The things I used to rush through became worth slowing down for, because I was doing them alongside you.


"You are the kind of person who makes the world feel like a place worth showing up to -- every single day."


I love the way you think -- sharp and curious and wonderfully strange. I love how you laugh before you finish your own joke. I love the kindness you carry without even realising it.


On this day, I hope you feel every bit of how loved you are. Not just by me -- though I plan to make that abundantly clear -- but by everyone whose life is better because you exist in it.


Here is to you, my love. Today and every day that follows. 🥂

- always yours
